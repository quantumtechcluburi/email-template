Recap · Navigating the Quantum Era

Key takeaways from Barbara Widholm

Quantum in finance: Barbara shared how organizations, including financial services, are exploring quantum computing, and where the line sits between what is real today and future potential.

Optimization problems: Her resources center on D-Wave's quantum annealers, applied to the Traveling Salesman Problem and to portfolio optimization, including a 40-stock portfolio.

Learn by doing: She pointed to hackathons including MIT iQuHACK (with a State Street × Classiq challenge) and YQuantum at Yale. Its AWS × State Street challenge tested whether quantum-derived features could beat strong classical baselines at predicting S&P 500 excess returns.

Skills and careers: Barbara shared her own journey into AI and quantum computing, the skills you can start building now, and ways to get involved through clubs, research, certifications, and networking.
