Quantum Technologies Club at the University of Rhode Island

Hi everyone,

Thank you to everyone who came to our intro meeting on Tuesday! We were thrilled by the turnout and the curiosity in the room. QTC@URI is URI's first student-led quantum club, and seeing so many of you show up, from every major and with no prior quantum background, is exactly what we hoped for. We're excited to build this community with you.

 

Quick recap

Why we exist: Quantum is booming, but undergrad classes offer little exposure to it. We're a relaxed space to learn, hang out, and meet like-minded people. No background needed, just curiosity.

What we do: Share learning resources, run quantum workshops, participate in external hackathons, host guest speakers from academia and industry, and build an interdisciplinary STEM community.

Our team: Damian Lapena Vidal (President), Ahmad Junaid (Vice President), Ben Buccheri (Operations & Finance Chair), and Simeon Larinde (Programs & Communication Chair), with Dr. Irene Andreu (Chemical Engineering) as our faculty advisor.
