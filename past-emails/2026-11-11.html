Recap · Qiskit from Zero

What we covered

Notebook 01 · Meet the Qubit: qubits as arrows on the Bloch sphere, the X, H, Z, and rotation gates that move them around.

Notebook 02 · Measure & Simulate: measurement and randomness, shots and histograms, the "peek" experiment, and interference fringes (a double-slit with a single qubit).

IBM Quantum account: we walked through creating a free account, which includes access to real quantum computers (QPUs), so you are ready to run your own circuits on real hardware.
 

Workshop materials

All files live in the club's GitHub repo. No install needed: the notebooks open straight in Google Colab, and each one is saved with its outputs, so you can read it like a book.

Tip: notebooks 01 to 03 need no account. Notebook 04 needs the free IBM Quantum account many of you set up on Tuesday. If you have not yet, the setup guide walks you through it step by step. The free plan includes 10 minutes of quantum computer time every 28 days.
