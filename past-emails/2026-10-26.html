Good news after last week's change of plans: we now have two guest talks lined up for the next two Tuesdays. Today at 5:00 PM, Dr. Chris Lirakis joins us in person to talk about the future of quantum computing. Barbara Widholm's talk, originally planned for today, is now next Tuesday, November 3. Today · Guest speaker

Future of Quantum Computing

Date and Time: Tuesday, October 27, 2026 @ 5:00 – 6:00 PM

Speaker: Dr. Chris Lirakis

Role: Owner, LirakisQuantum · Adjunct Physics Professor, URI

Location: Engineering Building Room 040 or online (Hybrid)
*Speaker will be joining in person.

What Chris will cover:

  • A brief history of quantum computing and how it relates to classical development.
  • How big the system picture is, and why all disciplines need to be involved.
  • What is involved in making a superconducting quantum computer.
  • An introduction to quantum algorithms.
  • Open Q&A.

Open to all URI students, faculty and staff, any major. Bring a friend!

Navigating the Quantum Era: Finance, Innovation, and Career Pathways

Date and Time: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 @ 5:00 – 6:00 PM

Speaker: Barbara Widholm

Role and Company: Emerging Tech Governance Lead, State Street

Location: Engineering Building Room 040 or online (Hybrid)

What Barbara will cover:

  • Her career journey into AI and quantum computing.
  • Why there is so much excitement around quantum computing.

  • What is real today versus future potential.
  • Skills and career paths you can start building now.
  • Getting involved: clubs, hackathons, research, certifications, and networking.
